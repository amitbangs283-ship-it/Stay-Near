About Stay Near

Finding a room should feel less uncertain.

Stay Near is a proposed student accommodation guide designed to make early housing research easier and more organised.

Why we are here

A clearer way to begin your accommodation search.

Students looking for a place near campus may have to rely on scattered recommendations and incomplete details. It can be difficult to compare location, price and facilities consistently.

Stay Near proposes a simple, mobile-friendly directory with searchable listings, clear contact steps and a viewing checklist. The service supports discovery; it does not guarantee a property or replace an in-person check.

Our purpose

Who Stay Near is designed to serve

Three groups who can benefit from clearer accommodation information.

🎓

Students

New and returning students who need affordable options, a manageable commute and clear facility information.

♡

Families

Families supporting a student and helping them shortlist options before arranging a visit.

⌂

Accommodation providers

Providers who want to share useful, straightforward information about rooms and facilities.

A student-first approach

Search, shortlist, then verify.

Our goal is to make the early stages less overwhelming without pretending every listing is guaranteed or verified.
