Before you decide

A simple room-viewing checklist

Take your time and make sure the details are clear before you agree to anything.

Confirm availability

Contact the provider, confirm the room is still available and arrange a viewing.

Inspect the space

Check facilities, water and electricity, access, security arrangements and the surrounding area.

Review the terms

Ask for the full cost, deposit, agreement and notice terms. Do not pay before verifying the provider and terms.

This prototype is an information concept, not legal, financial or safety advice. Bring someone you trust to a viewing where possible.
