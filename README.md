*PROJECT OVERVIEW: Stay Near*
*Stay Near is a prototype concept for a Student Accommodation Finder in Sierra Leone.* It is NOT a live booking site. All listings, prices, photos and contacts are illustrative samples to show how the idea would work. The goal is to help students find a place near campus and prepare to view it with more confidence.

*Page Breakdown:*

*1. Home Page - (A place to feel at home)*
This is the landing page. It introduces the idea: find student-friendly rooms near campus, compare location, room type and essentials before you visit.
It has a simple search bar (by area and room type) and explains the 3-step student-first process:
- Browse rooms
- Check the location
- Visit with confidence
It also shows 3 Featured Accommodation Examples and ends with a checklist: Compare types and costs, Know what to ask, Keep the final decision in your hands.

*2. About Page - (Finding a room should feel less uncertain)*
This page explains the problem and the purpose. Students struggle with scattered recommendations and incomplete details.
Stay Near is a simple, mobile-friendly directory with searchable listings, contact steps and a viewing checklist. It does NOT guarantee a property or replace an in-person check.
It is designed to serve 3 groups: Students, Families, and Accommodation Providers. The approach is: Search, shortlist, then verify.

*3. Find a Room Page - (Find a room that fits your student life)*
This is the searchable directory page. It has filters for area/keyword, room type, and budget. It clearly states all listings are sample entries to demonstrate the prototype.
It shows 6 sample listings:
- Bright shared room in Hill Station
- Private room near central Freetown
- Student residence near campus
- Shared student apartment
- Compact furnished room
- Quiet residence-style room
Each has an "Ask provider" and "Ask about a room" button.
At the bottom, it has a guide: "Before you agree to a room" - questions about Total monthly cost, Utilities and facilities, and Agreement and access.

*4. Services Page - (Tools for the whole search)*
This page lists the practical tools the platform provides:
- *Room directory:* Explore sample room types and what to ask.
- *Location planning:* Consider campus distance, transport and everyday services.
- *Viewing checklist:* A simple checklist before you decide - 01 Confirm availability, 02 Inspect the space (water, electricity, security), 03 Review the terms (deposit, agreement).
It also has a disclaimer that this is an information concept, not legal or financial advice.

*5. Contact Page - (Questions or a listing to share?)*
This page is for contacting the concept team. Left side shows info: Email is hello@staynear.example (placeholder), Office hours Mon-Fri 9-16, Location focus Sierra Leone.
Right side has a demo form: Name, Email, Message about, and Message box. The form states it works on the page but does not send or store data, because this is a prototype.

*Footer for all pages:*
Same message everywhere: Helping students in Sierra Leone explore options, compare essentials and prepare for a viewing with more confidence.

A responsive multi-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype with teal, white and sky-blue styling.

## Run in VS Code
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the `Stay-Near-Student-Accommodation-Finder` folder in VS Code.
3. Install the **Live Server** extension if you want local auto-refresh.
4. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**. You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser.

## Notes
- All navigation links are buttons/pill-style links and each page shares the same header and footer.
- The photos load from Unsplash, so an internet connection is required to display them.
- Listing information, prices, facilities and the email address are illustrative placeholders, not verified offers.
- The contact form only displays a demo confirmation; it does not send or save messages. A real launch needs a secure form backend.
